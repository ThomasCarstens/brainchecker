How a status changes (the protocol, from your form)
  • Every line is presumed AI-written and unexamined until there is evidence otherwise. Claude's own edits count as unexamined until you mark them.
  • Examined means reproduced another way, your own rationale, a second reviewer, a checked source, or a walkthrough someone else can follow. Tests passing, "it looked right", and an AI explanation do not count. Specifying a change is not examining it.
  • Critical or passable comes from the path map, and a few conditions raise it: an equation, a parameter, a tolerance or clamp, what is compared, a claim in a comment, a deleted gate, a new call sequence in the plotter.
  • Report passages appear as evidence to confirm. They never mark a decision examined on their own: K28 to K30 are written up in reports, and you still answered "Don't know" for them.
  • The AI note (Opus 5, dashed box) predicts what a decision could move. It is never an examination, and a check it suggests is shown here but never run.
  • Only your marks change a status. Pick Examined and say how you checked it, or pick Mine or Not a decision. Anyone else's "examined" stays a proposal in the thread.
